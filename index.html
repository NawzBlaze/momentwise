Your time is yours. Morrow is being designed to help you pause before falling into another endless scroll and choose how you want to spend your time.

A gentle breath before the habit loops. Friction where you need awareness, ease everywhere else.
The challenge

Mindless reflex, replaced with a mindful choice.

The involuntary loop

Most screen time does not begin with an intention. It starts in an idle second: picking up the phone to check one thing, opening an algorithmic feed on muscle memory, and looking up forty minutes later wondering where the time went.

Traditional blockers treat screen time like a failure of willpower, relying on aggressive lockouts that invite resentment, bypasses, or guilt.

Morrow’s direction

Morrow is being shaped around a gentler thesis: you do not need an authority figure in your pocket—you need a split second to remember what you actually care about.

By introducing gentle interruptions, thoughtful choice prompts, and respectful reminders at the threshold of distracting apps, Morrow aims to give your conscious mind space to step in.

Guiding principles

Design goals, from day one.

These commitments guide every decision as Morrow is designed. They reflect our intended standards, not claims of current software features.

01

Intentional, not judgmental

Technology should never shame you. Morrow will not use punitive language, streak counters that induce anxiety, or arbitrary punishments. It exists to foster self-awareness, not guilt.

02

Helpful, not controlling

You always stay in command of your device. If you genuinely want to open an app, you can. Morrow offers a quiet buffer to confirm your intent, never an adversarial barrier.

03

Private by design

Your app usage, habits, and daily choices belong strictly to you. Morrow will be architected around on-device processing with zero data brokers, no behavioral advertising, and no tracking.

04

Simple enough to stay out of the way

A tool meant to reduce screen time should not become another app you spend hours managing. Configuration should be minimal, lightweight, and calm.

Make room for what matters.

Morrow is in early development. We are taking the time to design an experience that genuinely respects your attention and autonomy.
