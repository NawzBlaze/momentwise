Android Platform Architecture

Respectful by Design

In the production Android app, leaving an app returns you cleanly to your device home screen. Momentwise respects user autonomy and platform conventions—it never forcibly terminates background tasks.

Proposed Android Architecture

In the planned Android release, all rules and reminder schedules will remain strictly on your device with no tracking or remote telemetry. This web prototype stores demo settings in your browser's LocalStorage only.
